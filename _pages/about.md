I am a Master's student in Quantum Information at **Sorbonne University** in Paris, France. My research focuses on quantum dynamics, quantum nonlocality, convex optimization, and machine learning pipelines applied to quantum systems.

Prior to Sorbonne University, I earned a Bachelor of Computer Science with an Artificial Intelligence specialization at **Charles University** in Prague, where I conducted research on adversarial robustness in deep neural networks and high-performance computing (HPC) workflows.

### Research Interests
- **Quantum Nonlocality & Correlations**: Classifying steerability and Local Hidden States (LHS), discovering multipartite Bell inequalities using polytope vertex annotation and linear programming.
- **Optimization in Quantum Systems**: Convex optimization (MOSEK, CVXPY), see-saw methods, and SDPs applied to quantum state classification and resource theories.
- **Quantum Error Correction & Fault Tolerance**: Applying AI and mathematical optimization to autonomous quantum error correction and fault-tolerant quantum computing architectures.
