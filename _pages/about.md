I am a Master’s student in Quantum Information at Sorbonne University in Paris, France. My research focuses on quantum dynamics, quantum nonlocality, convex optimisation, and machine learning pipelines applied to quantum systems.

Before Sorbonne University, I earned a Bachelor of Computer Science with an Artificial Intelligence specialisation at Charles University in Prague, where I did research on adversarial robustness in deep neural networks and high-performance computing (HPC) workflows.

### Research Interests
- **Quantum Error Correction & Fault Tolerance**: Applying mathematical optimisation to autonomous quantum error correction and fault-tolerant quantum computing architectures.
- **Quantum Nonlocality & Correlations**: Classifying steerability and Local Hidden States (LHS), discovering multipartite Bell inequalities using polytope vertex annotation and linear programming.
- **Optimisation in Quantum Systems**: Convex optimisation (MOSEK, CVXPY), see-saw methods, and SDPs applied to quantum state classification and resource theories.
