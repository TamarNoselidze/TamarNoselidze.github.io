### Overview

**Long Swim to Freedom** is the official web platform and digital hub for a documentary expedition tackling two urgent, interconnected crises in rural KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa: the epidemic of child drownings during school commutes and the devastating surge in rhino poaching threatening endangered wildlife.

The platform serves as the central digital communication vehicle to mobilise international awareness, connect non-governmental organisations (NGOs), and channel direct philanthropic contributions to grassroots swimming programmes and frontline anti-poaching ranger units.

- **Stack**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (jQuery), Formspree
- **Design Architecture**: Responsive single-page layout adapted from HTML5 UP *Story*
- **GitHub Repository**: [TamarNoselidze/Long-Swim-to-Freedom](https://github.com/TamarNoselidze/Long-Swim-to-Freedom)

### Expedition Mission & Dual Focus

The project addresses two connected humanitarian and ecological challenges centred around the **iSimangaliso Wetland Park**, a UNESCO World Heritage site:

- **The Human Crisis (Water Safety & Drowning Prevention)**: In rural KwaZulu-Natal, many children must traverse dangerous bodies of water on their daily commute to school. Lacking fundamental swimming skills and safe crossings, students risk drowning simply to obtain an education. The campaign advocates for vital community-based swimming education and aquatic safety infrastructure.
- **The Conservation Crisis (Rhino Anti-Poaching)**: Driven by the illegal wildlife trade, intense poaching has brought rhinos under critical threat. The documentary documents the harsh realities on the ground and provides direct support to the anti-poaching units and rangers fighting to protect vulnerable populations.

---

### Key Website Objectives & Architecture

- **Public Education & Advocacy**: Educates international audiences on human–wildlife coexistence and water safety realities in rural South Africa.
- **Showcase of Biodiversity**: Highlights the unique ecosystems and rich wildlife of the iSimangaliso Wetland Park through dedicated interactive spotlight sections.
- **Serverless Form Handling**: Integrates **Formspree** to manage backend routing for the "Partner With Us" contact portal without requiring a dedicated application server.
- **Smooth Interactive Experience**: Utilises jQuery for smooth-scrolling navigation, scroll-fade animations, and responsive mobile menu toggling.
- **Impact & Crowdfunding**: Directs visitors towards crowdfunding channels and verified partners supporting frontline anti-poaching units and rural aquatic programmes.
